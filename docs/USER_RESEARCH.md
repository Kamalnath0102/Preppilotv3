# User research kit

## Who to talk to (aim for 5 to 8)
Final-year students, recent graduates and career switchers preparing for tech interviews. Mix of confident and anxious candidates.

## 20-minute interview script
1. How are you preparing for interviews right now? Walk me through your last week.
2. Tell me about your last mock or real interview. What happened?
3. What is the hardest part: knowing what to study, practicing, or getting feedback?
4. What have you tried (YouTube, friends, platforms, mentors)? What did you like or dislike?
5. Have you used AI for interview practice? Did you trust the feedback? Why or why not?
6. _(Let them use PrepPilot for one session while thinking aloud. Do not explain anything.)_
7. Where did you hesitate or get confused? What surprised you?
8. Did the score feel fair? What would make you trust it more?
9. What would make you come back next week? Would you pay for anything here? What?

## Google Form (for quick follow-up, 2 minutes)
1. How do you prepare for interviews today? (multi-select)
2. How realistic did the questions feel? (1 to 5, plus why)
3. How fair did the scores feel? (1 to 5, plus why)
4. Which part was most useful? (feedback / missed points / follow-up / 7-day plan)
5. How likely are you to use this again next week? (1 to 5)
6. What is the one thing you would change?
